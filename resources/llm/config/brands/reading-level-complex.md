# Complex reading level

Use nuanced vocabulary and domain terminology naturally for an experienced reader, without obscuring the meaning.

## How to write

- Allow precise distinctions and layered explanation when they help answer the message's one job.
- Use terminology accurately and define it when a capable general reader may not share the same specialty.
- Favor controlled nuance over unnecessary length or ornamental prose.
- Connect details to the reader's stated context so sophistication remains useful rather than performative.
- Keep the core point visible even when the sentence structure carries more texture.

## Avoid

Do not use jargon to imply authority, bury a limitation, or make a simple point sound more certain. Complexity is a presentation choice; it cannot introduce clinical inference, unsupported claims, or a different offer.
