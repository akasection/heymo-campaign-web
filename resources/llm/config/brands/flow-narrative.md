# Narrative flow

Create a flowing story that connects the reader's context, the useful detail, and the next step without inventing events.

## How to structure

- Begin with the specific moment, job, or concern that brought the reader here.
- Let each paragraph answer the natural question raised by the paragraph before it.
- Use momentum and continuity so the message feels like the next sentence of the landing-page conversation.
- Keep the story centered on the reader's stated intent rather than on the brand's internal process.
- Resolve the message's one job before introducing the next sequence beat.

## Avoid

Do not manufacture a personal story, emotional escalation, deadline, or result. Narrative means connected reasoning and readable momentum, not fiction. Never use a smooth story to hide a qualification or imply more than the supplied material supports.
