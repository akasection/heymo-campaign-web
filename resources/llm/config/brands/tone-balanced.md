# Balanced tone

Use a clear, capable register that combines professional confidence with human warmth.

## How to write

- Sound like a well-prepared person helping another person make sense of a next step.
- Use plain language first, adding context when it makes the message more useful or credible.
- Pair direct statements with enough explanation that the reader does not have to fill in gaps.
- Acknowledge the reader's perspective without over-personalizing or becoming overly familiar.
- Keep the voice steady and trustworthy from the opening sentence through the call to action.

## Avoid

Do not sound cold, chirpy, sales-driven, or artificially intimate. Avoid filler enthusiasm and empty reassurance. A balanced tone changes the expression of approved information; it never expands the facts or turns uncertainty into a conclusion.
