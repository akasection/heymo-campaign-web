# Formal tone

Use a precise, professional register that feels composed, accountable, and deliberate.

## How to write

- Address the reader as a capable adult and explain the reason for each important point.
- Prefer complete sentences, exact verbs, and measured transitions over casual shorthand.
- Let confidence come from clarity and evidence, not from certainty that the available facts do not support.
- Use courteous language without sounding deferential, bureaucratic, or distant.
- Keep the emotional temperature calm, especially when acknowledging a reader's concern.

## Avoid

Do not use slang, internet shorthand, exaggerated enthusiasm, sales patter, or jokes. Do not turn formality into dense legalistic language. Do not let a formal register add a clinical conclusion, recommendation, or promise that the supplied material does not contain.
