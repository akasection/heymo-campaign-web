# Informal tone

Use direct, natural language that sounds like a thoughtful peer speaking plainly.

## How to write

- Prefer familiar words and a conversational rhythm without becoming careless.
- Speak to the reader as "you" when the source context supports it, and keep the focus on what they told us.
- Use contractions sparingly when they make a sentence sound natural and remain easy to understand.
- Lead with the useful point, then add the context that helps the reader act with confidence.
- Make the message feel approachable through honesty, not through forced jokes or trendy slang.

## Avoid

Do not use hype, banter, emojis, pressure, or a fake friendship. Do not make an informal voice sound less accurate. Keep every fact, qualification, and boundary exactly as supplied by the system.
