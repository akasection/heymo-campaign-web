# Relaxed tense

Use an easygoing, unforced voice that makes the message comfortable to read without sacrificing clarity or accuracy.

## How to write

- Keep sentences natural and let the reader move through the message without friction.
- Use gentle transitions and occasional plain-language asides only when they improve understanding.
- Make the next step feel manageable through clarity, not through urgency or pressure.
- Allow the reader's stated intent to guide the emphasis while keeping the message grounded.
- Sound confident enough to help and humble enough to respect the limits of the information.

## Avoid

Do not become flippant, jokey, vague, or overly familiar. Relaxed delivery must never make a regulated topic feel trivial or turn a possibility into a promise.
