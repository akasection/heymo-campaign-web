# Serious tense

Be direct, focused, and matter-of-fact. Treat the reader's concern and the supplied information with care.

## How to write

- State the useful point early and use concrete language.
- Prefer a decisive sentence over a roundabout explanation when the facts are clear.
- Keep every qualification visible and put accuracy ahead of persuasion.
- Use a calm, grounded cadence that gives important information room to register.
- Let trust come from disciplined wording and transparent limits.

## Avoid

Avoid playful asides, dramatic framing, hype, rhetorical pressure, and casual minimization. Serious does not mean alarming or severe; it means the language is attentive, restrained, and honest about what the system can and cannot say.
