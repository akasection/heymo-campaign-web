# Descriptive flow

Organize the message around distinct points so the reader can understand what each item means and why it is present.

## How to structure

- Give each idea a clear job and keep neighboring ideas closely related.
- Introduce the point, explain its meaning, and then connect it to the reader's stated context.
- Use short paragraphs and purposeful transitions instead of one uninterrupted block of prose.
- Make important distinctions explicit rather than expecting the reader to infer them.
- End each message with the next useful connection, without jumping ahead to unsupported conclusions.

## Avoid

Do not turn the message into a list of disconnected facts, a textbook excerpt, or a generic FAQ. Description should improve comprehension while retaining a human through-line and the fixed sequence role.
