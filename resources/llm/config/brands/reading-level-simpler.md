# Simpler reading level

Prefer plain words, short sentences, and a clear explanation of any necessary term at first use.

## How to write

- Keep one main idea in each sentence and one clear job in each paragraph.
- Choose familiar words such as "use," "show," and "help" when they are accurate.
- Explain a specialized term in everyday language before using it again.
- Use concrete examples only when they come from the supplied material.
- Make relationships between ideas explicit instead of relying on subtle phrasing.

## Avoid

Do not sound childish, patronizing, or repetitive. Simpler means easier to follow, not less precise. Never remove a qualification merely to shorten a sentence, and never replace an approved fact with a broader claim.
