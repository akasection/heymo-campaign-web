# Balanced flow

Use a clear structure with a natural through-line between the reader's context, the useful detail, and the next step.

## How to structure

- Open with the point that matters most for this message's sequence role.
- Move from context to explanation in a way that feels orderly but not mechanical.
- Use transitions to show why the next paragraph follows from the previous one.
- Give the reader enough detail to feel oriented, then stop before the message becomes exhaustive.
- Preserve a single central emphasis rather than competing for attention with unrelated points.

## Avoid

Do not force a story arc where the material does not support one, and do not flatten the message into isolated bullet points. The flow may guide attention, but it cannot change approved facts or the required call to action.
