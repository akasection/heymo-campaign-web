# Balanced reading level

Use natural professional vocabulary that remains easy to scan and explain specialized terms when they matter.

## How to write

- Mix familiar language with the domain terms needed to be accurate.
- Introduce a technical phrase with a short explanation rather than assuming expertise.
- Vary sentence length enough to keep the message natural while preserving quick comprehension.
- Use precise distinctions when they help the reader understand the purpose of the message.
- Keep the prose polished without making it sound academic or simplified beyond recognition.

## Avoid

Do not use jargon as decoration, dense abstractions, or unexplained abbreviations. Reading level affects accessibility and rhythm; it does not change the underlying facts, boundaries, or next step.
